About

ScholarChat is a multi-domain academic RAG chatbot built over your course materials (NLP, Cloud Computing, Pakistan Studies, and Sealed Nectar). It retrieves the most relevant documents using FAISS + E5 embeddings, then generates a context-grounded answer with citations.

Tips

  • Ask specific questions for better retrieval.
  • Check “Sources” under answers for citations.
  • Long answers may take time—keep this tab open.
